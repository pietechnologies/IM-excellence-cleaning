Still have a question? We’re happy to help.

What’s included in a standard clean?

Our residential cleaning covers everyday dusting, surface care, kitchen and bathroom sanitizing, vacuuming, and mopping. We’ll confirm the exact scope with your quote.

Can I book a one-time cleaning?

Absolutely. Choose a one-time clean for a refresh, a move, or a special occasion. Weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly cleaning options are also available.

How do I get a quote and confirm a booking?

Complete our two-step form to tell us about your space. Our team confirms pricing, service availability, and your preferred date before an appointment is booked.

How does the new-client offer work?

New clients can request 10% off their first cleaning. Mention the offer in your enquiry. It cannot be combined with other offers; our team will confirm the details with your quote.
